Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Scientific Papers

Independent researchSept 2026 – presentPaper in preparation

  • Compared image-based (CLIP, jina-embeddings-v4) and text-based figure retrieval for research-paper QA: developed on 10 papers, tested once on 5 unseen papers with frozen settings (113 questions, 3 LLMs, significance tests).

How Little Data Does a Small Model Need to Reason?

Data-efficient training and adaptive reasoning in small TransformersOngoing

  • Studies how the size, source and selection of training data affect reasoning in a small Transformer trained from scratch on Sudoku, across five research questions.
  • Built a reproducible pipeline: 111,000 puzzles sampled from 3M, validity-preserving augmentation, and a balanced easy/medium/hard test set of 3,000.
  • An 806k-parameter one-pass baseline memorised at 10k puzzles and solved 0% of puzzles even at 98k, which led to building a looping reasoning model next.

Built with Python, PyTorch, NumPy, Kaggle / Colab
